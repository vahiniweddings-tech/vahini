What kind of photography do you offer?

Our services cover weddings, receptions, pre-wedding photography, couple portraits and family celebrations. Contact the studio to confirm coverage for your event.

Can I enquire about both photography and videography?

Yes. Tell us what you have in mind when you get in touch, so the team can discuss the available options.

How early should I book?

Availability depends on the date and event schedule. Contact the studio once your event date is confirmed.
